Offset Multiplier (export units needed per import unit)

Normal export offsets Normal import 1:1 first. Any leftover export then offsets Peak import, then Off-Peak — each at its own exchange rate. E.g. 1.25 for Peak means 1.25 units of leftover export are needed to cancel 1 unit of Peak import.
